Flagship Hardware Project

5-DOF Precision Articulated Robotic Manipulator

Engineered from the ground up by the SJBIT Robotics research team at the Department of Electronics & Communication Engineering. Designed for high-repeatability pick-and-place routines, spatial trajectory solving, and multi-axis kinematics.

  • In-House Additive Manufacturing: High-infill PLA custom printed articulated joints, base carousel, and low-deflection bicep/forearm beams.
  • Integrated End-Effector: Dual-finger parallel gripper driven by direct-linkage micro servo with compliance pads for delicate payload manipulation.
  • Harmonic Cable Loom: Clean low-friction wire routing channels preventing joint pinching across full 180° rotation envelopes.
  • Kinematic Control: Closed-form analytical Inverse Kinematics (IK) running on 32-bit ARM Cortex embedded controller.

IMPULSE 2026

Organized by Department of ECE, SJBIT Bangalore

The SJBIT Robotics Club was formally felicitated and awarded the prestigious Certificate of Excellence during IMPULSE 2026, hosted by the Department of Electronics & Communication Engineering at SJB Institute of Technology, Bangalore.

  • Faculty & Departmental Honors: Presented in the presence of distinguished faculty mentors, senior professors, and Head of Department, recognizing exemplary innovation.
  • Technical Demonstration: Honored for groundbreaking student-led execution of autonomous robotic hardware, precision manipulators, and firmware design.
  • Multidisciplinary Impact: Showcasing seamless convergence between electrical drive systems, CAD fabrication, and real-time control algorithms.

Core Technical Wings

Engineering Domains at SJBIT Robotics

Hands-on incubation, peer mentorship, and competitive robotics development powered by state-of-the-art ECE lab infrastructure.

01 // KINEMATICS & MECHANISMS

Manipulators & Arms

Forward and inverse kinematics, 3D printed cycloidal gearboxes, servo teleoperation, and precision gripper design.

02 // EMBEDDED ARCHITECTURE

Firmware & Microcontrollers

Bare-metal C/C++, FreeRTOS on ARM Cortex-M, ESP32 dual-core IoT telemetry, CAN communication, and motor drives.

03 // PERCEPTION & EDGE AI

Computer Vision

Real-time object segmentation, OpenCV tracking, depth camera calibration, and embedded neural networks on edge accelerators.

04 // RAPID PROTOTYPING

Additive Fabrication

SolidWorks parametric CAD modeling, FDM 3D printing with engineering filament (PLA/PETG/ABS), and rapid iterative testing.
